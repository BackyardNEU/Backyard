Privacy Policy

Introduction

Backyard ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at explorethebackyard.com. This policy explains how we handle your personal data when you sign up for our launch waitlist. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

What We Collect

We collect only your email address, provided voluntarily through our newsletter signup form.

Why We Collect It

We use your email address solely to send you launch announcements and occasional product updates about Backyard. We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Store It

Email addresses are stored securely by Resend, a third-party email service provider based in the United States. Resend maintains industry-standard security practices to protect your data.

Consent

We only collect your email address when you explicitly opt in by completing the signup form and checking the consent box. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

Your Rights

  • Unsubscribe: Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Click it to stop receiving emails immediately.
  • Deletion: To request complete removal of your data, email us at explorethebackyard2025@gmail.com.
  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you by contacting us at the same email address.

Third-Party Services

  • Resend — email delivery and contact management
  • Vercel — website hosting

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies on this website.

Children's Privacy

Backyard is intended for university students aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at explorethebackyard2025@gmail.com.
